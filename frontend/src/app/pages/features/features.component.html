Capabilities

Platform Features

Everything needed to track, monitor, and govern departmental budgets from allocation through to expenditure.

💰

Budget Allocation & Management

Create annual or quarterly allocations, assign funds to departments and schemes, and modify allocations as plans evolve.

🧾

Expenditure Tracking

Record transactions with category tagging, supporting documentation uploads, and status workflows.

🤖

Monitoring & Detection Engine

Calculates real-time utilization %, flags under-utilization, overspending, and spending spikes.

🔔

Alerts & Reporting

Automated alerts with severity levels, department-wise summaries, and downloadable CSV reports.

⚙

Admin Controls

Configure threshold rules, manage departments and users, and review full audit logs.

📊

Visual Dashboards

Bar charts, trend lines, and category breakdowns built with Chart.js for fast comprehension.
