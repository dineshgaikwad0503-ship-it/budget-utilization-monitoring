Context & Mission

About the System

Traditional budget monitoring relies on periodic manual reporting and disconnected spreadsheets, which delays visibility into spending patterns. This platform replaces that with continuous, real-time monitoring.

🏛

Built for Governance

Modeled on financial governance patterns referenced by the CAG of India and the Open Budget Portal (International Budget Partnership).

🎯

Problem It Solves

Lack of real-time visibility, delayed identification of under-utilized funds, and limited transparency across departments.

📈

Data-Driven Decisions

Continuous evaluation of allocation vs. actual expenditure supports timely corrective action.

🤝

Collaborating Organization

Developed in reference to Unified Mentor project guidelines for financial governance platforms.
